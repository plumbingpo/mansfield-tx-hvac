Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Houston

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

The sooner you call about a comfort problem, the smaller the repair tends to be. A capacitor that's starting to fail costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement.
